About Zoxel

Zoxel isn't just another game platform. We're building something fresh, something different. No Roblox assets. No Roblox name. Just pure, creative gameplay.

Join a server, chat with friends, and start building your world. Our client runs on Rust with the Bevy game engine, delivering smooth 60+ FPS gameplay.

FAQ

When will Zoxel launch?

We're targeting Q3 2026 for our website and devlogs launch, with bigger play tests in Q4 2026. You can stay updated by following our social media.

What platforms will Zoxel support?

We'll start with Windows, then expand to macOS, Linux, and eventually mobile devices including iOS and Android.

Is Zoxel free to play?

Yes! Zoxel will be free to play with optional in-game purchases for premium items and features.
